6. The Always On Time assistant connector

The free, optional connector at https://alwaysontime.zombit.io/mcp lets an assistant you connect through OAuth read your upcoming alarms, synced alarm settings, and recent alarm history, and change supported alarm settings. It cannot read or change your calendar directly. Connecting grants ongoing access until disconnected; it does not require a new approval in our app for every action. On Android 16 and later, the optional Gemini integration can read alarm information and change supported settings on your phone without needing our connector or account sync. You can turn it off in Settings › Connected assistants. These assistant features are subject to the alarm warning in section 7 and the assistant provider's own terms and privacy policy.

  • It is free and optional. Nothing is shared until you connect an assistant and approve it with your Always On Time account.
  • AI assistants can misunderstand you and may send unintended changes, including changes that silence or pause alarms. Check the affected phone to confirm what actually changed; a change saved by the connector may not reach an offline phone until it reconnects. Changes are logged for 30 days and can be undone while the record remains available, unless those settings have changed since. Disconnecting the last assistant deletes the connector's change log. Undo cannot recover a missed alarm.
  • You can disconnect at any time in the app (Settings › Connected assistants) or in your assistant. Connections that go unused for 90 days are disconnected automatically.
  • We may limit how often the connector can be used, and we may change, suspend, or stop it.
  • Your assistant's provider handles your conversations under its own terms and privacy policy. See section 9.
