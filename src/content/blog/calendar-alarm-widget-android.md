Always On Time's home screen widgets show your upcoming events together with **when each alarm will ring**, so you can see the rest of your day, and whether anything is set to ring, without opening an app. There are three: **Today**, **Cards** and **Next up**. Tap any event to turn its alarm off or on.

We make Always On Time. These widgets are part of its Android app, which turns calendar events into full-screen alarms.

## Pick a widget

- **Today** lists your day in order. Each row shows the event, where it is and when its alarm rings. It starts at 4 × 4 cells.
- **Cards** shows the same day as separate cards, which are easier to scan at a glance. It also starts at 4 × 4.
- **Next up** is the small one, at 2 × 2. It shows your next event and when it rings, and as you make it bigger it fills in the rest of the day.

All three can be resized. They follow the calendars you chose in the app, so a calendar you don't watch won't clutter them.

## Add one to your home screen

1. **Install Always On Time** from Google Play, allow calendar access and choose which calendars to watch.
2. **Touch and hold an empty spot** on your home screen, then tap **Widgets**.
3. **Find Always On Time** and drag **Today**, **Cards** or **Next up** onto the screen.
4. **Resize it** by touching and holding it, then dragging its edges.

The wording varies a little between phone makers; [Google's Android Help](https://support.google.com/android/answer/9450271) covers the general steps.

## What the widget tells you

Each event carries a short note about its alarm:

- **Rings 9:50**: when the alarm will go off.
- **Leave 9:20**: for an event with travel alarms, when it's time to head out, from your [time-to-leave alarm](/blog/google-calendar-travel-time-android/).
- **Alarm off** or **Paused**: this event won't ring, either because you turned its alarm off or because alarms are paused.

If alarms are paused, the widget says so and when they come back. If something would stop alarms from ringing, such as a missing permission, the widget shows **Alarms may not ring. Tap to fix.** and takes you to the fix.

When an event has a Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams or Webex link, a **Join** button can appear beside it, so you can open the call straight from the home screen.

## Change an alarm from the widget

Tap an event to open a small sheet over your home screen:

- **Alarm for this event** turns that event's alarm off or on. For a recurring meeting it changes just this one; open the event in the app to change the whole series. If you change your mind, tap **Undo**.
- **Add another alarm** opens the event in the app, ready to add an extra ring, such as one five minutes before the start.
- **Open event** takes you to its full details in the app.

That makes the widget a quick way to quiet one meeting you're skipping without touching your calendar, or to add a ring for one that matters.
