Artillery Let Loose can take the target distance by voice. Turn on **Voice Entry** in Settings, tap the mic key and say the number, like "eight fifty". The calculator enters 850 m and shows the elevation to dial, so your hands can stay on the mouse and keyboard.

We make Artillery Let Loose. Voice Entry is a free, optional feature on iPhone and Android.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Settings** in Artillery Let Loose and turn on **Voice Entry**.
2. Read the short intro and tap **Continue**. The app downloads a 73 MB speech model once.
3. Allow microphone access when your phone asks.

A mic key now appears on the calculator keypad. Tap it to start listening and tap it again to stop.

## Say a number

Say the distance the way you'd call it out: "eight fifty", "nine twenty-five" or "twelve hundred". The hint above the keypad shows what it heard: **Entering 850 m — C cancels**.

The number waits a moment before it commits, three seconds by default. Tap **C** in that time to cancel it. You can change the pause under **Voice Commit Delay** (1.5, 3 or 5 seconds).

For self-propelled guns, switch to slope and say the vehicle's tilt, like "minus five".

Voice Entry understands numbers spoken in English.

## What it ignores

Voice Entry is built for a noisy match:

- **Numbers only.** It accepts a whole spoken number and ignores every other word, so a sentence around the number doesn't get typed.
- **In range only.** A distance outside your gun's range, or a slope beyond ±90°, shows a note and isn't entered.
- **On your phone.** Speech is recognized on the device and your audio doesn't leave it.
- **Turns itself off.** Listening stops after 30 seconds of quiet by default, and when you leave the screen or switch apps. Change it under **Voice Auto-Off**.

## Where it works

Voice Entry works in the Hell Let Loose calculator, self-propelled artillery (distance and slope), history mode and the WARDOGS calculator. With it off, the screens look as they did before.
