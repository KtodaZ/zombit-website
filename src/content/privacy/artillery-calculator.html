What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application has no accounts and does not ask you to register. Your saved configurations, targets, and labels are stored on your device. Settings are also stored on your device; whether History mode is enabled and whether the purchase is unlocked are additionally sent to Google Analytics for Firebase to understand feature use.

What information does the Application collect automatically?

In addition, the Application may collect certain information automatically, including, but not limited to, the type of mobile device you use, your mobile devices unique device ID, the IP address of your mobile device, your mobile operating system, the type of mobile Internet browsers you use, and information about the way you use the Application.

On both iOS and Android, the Application uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is used, for example which screens are opened and whether History mode or a purchase is used, and Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports. On Android, Google AdMob also collects information for advertising, as described below.

Google Analytics for Firebase uses an app instance identifier and may also collect the Android advertising ID when available. Firebase Crashlytics uses installation identifiers to associate crash reports with an installation. The Application does not ask you to provide your name or email address to these services.

Ads and purchases

iOS. The Application is a paid download on the App Store. It shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. Apple processes the payment, and the Service Provider does not receive your payment details.

Android. The Application is free on Google Play and shows ads served by Google AdMob: banner ads, full-screen ads at some transitions and when you return to the Application, and optional rewarded ads you can watch to unlock History mode for three hours. To serve, measure, and, where permitted, personalize ads, AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about your device and your interactions with ads. Where the law requires it, such as in the EEA and the UK, the Application asks for your consent with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice later from the Privacy Options link in the Application.

A one-time in-app purchase on Google Play unlocks History mode and removes ads. Google Play processes the payment. The Service Provider does not receive your payment details, and the Application stores on your device only whether the purchase is unlocked.
