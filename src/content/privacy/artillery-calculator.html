The short version

This policy covers the Artillery Let Loose app for mobile devices, listed as "ALL - HLL Artillery Calculator" on the App Store and "Wardogs & HLL Calculator" on Google Play. Zombit Studios LLC ("we") developed it as a commercial service and provides it "AS IS".

  • You don't need an account. Your saved configurations, targets, labels, and settings are stored on your device.
  • On iOS and Android, the app sends usage information to Google Analytics for Firebase and crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics. It does not currently have an in-app switch to turn these off.
  • Android only: the free app shows Google AdMob ads, which may use your advertising ID, IP address, and device information. Where the law requires it, such as in the EEA and the UK, you make your choice in Google's consent form and can change it later under Privacy Options. A one-time purchase removes ads.
  • On iOS, the app is a paid download with no ads and no in-app purchases. We don't receive your payment details.
  • The app does not collect precise location or use AI to process your data. It is not directed to anyone under 13.
  • Uninstalling the app stops all collection. To access or delete your information, or to opt out of sale or sharing for targeted advertising where US state law allows, email zombit.studios@gmail.com.

The details are below.

What you save in the app

The app has no accounts and does not ask you to register. Your saved configurations, targets, and labels are stored on your device. Settings are also stored on your device. Two of them, whether History mode is on and whether the purchase is unlocked, are also sent to Google Analytics for Firebase so we can understand feature use.

What the app collects automatically

The app may collect some information automatically, including, but not limited to, the type of mobile device you use, your device's unique device ID, its IP address, your mobile operating system, the type of mobile internet browsers you use, and information about how you use the app.

On both iOS and Android, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is used, for example which screens are opened and whether History mode or a purchase is used. It uses Firebase Crashlytics to receive crash reports. On Android, Google AdMob also collects information for advertising, as described below.

Google Analytics for Firebase uses an app instance identifier and may also collect the Android advertising ID when available. Firebase Crashlytics uses installation identifiers to link crash reports to an installation. The app does not ask you to give your name or email address to these services.

Ads and purchases

iOS. The app is a paid download on the App Store. It shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. Apple processes the payment, and we don't receive your payment details.

Android. The app is free on Google Play and shows ads served by Google AdMob: banner ads, full-screen ads at some transitions and when you return to the app, and optional rewarded ads you can watch to unlock History mode for three hours. To serve, measure, and, where permitted, personalize ads, AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about your device and your interactions with ads. Where the law requires it, such as in the EEA and the UK, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form. You can change your choice later from the Privacy Options link in the app.

A one-time in-app purchase on Google Play unlocks History mode and removes ads. Google Play processes the payment. We don't receive your payment details, and the app stores on your device only whether the purchase is unlocked.

Location

The app does not collect precise information about your device's location.

Artificial intelligence

The app does not use artificial intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

Who else receives your information

Usage and crash information is sent to Google's Firebase services to help us improve the app and our service. On Android, advertising information is sent to Google AdMob to show ads. We may share your information with third parties in the ways described in this policy.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob (Android only)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase
  • Firebase Crashlytics

We may disclose information you provide and information collected automatically:

  • as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
  • when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
  • with trusted service providers who work on our behalf, have no independent use of the information we disclose to them, and have agreed to follow the rules in this policy.

Your choices and rights

You can stop all collection by uninstalling the app. Use your device's standard uninstall process or the mobile app marketplace or network.

On Android, you can change your ad consent choice from the Privacy Options link in the app, where it is shown. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's settings.

Depending on where you are and the law that applies, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or get a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to its processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your local data protection authority. These rights can cover information collected automatically as well as information you provide. To make a request, email zombit.studios@gmail.com.

Where US state law gives you these rights, you can also opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising, without discrimination. Advertising disclosures to Google AdMob may count as sale or sharing under these laws. To opt out, email zombit.studios@gmail.com.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Saved configurations stay on your device until you delete them or remove the app's local data. We cannot delete these local records remotely.

Google keeps Analytics information according to this project's retention settings. Identifier retention may restart with new activity, and aggregate reports may be kept longer. Firebase Crashlytics keeps crash reports and their identifiers for 90 days before it begins removing them from live and backup systems. Google's retention practices also apply to advertising information processed by AdMob.

To request deletion of personal information processed by us or on our behalf, email zombit.studios@gmail.com. We may need more information to identify the relevant records.
