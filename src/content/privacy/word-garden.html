The details are below.

What the app collects

You don't need to register. Progress and settings stay on your device or in your browser. The word-list server and the web services described below receive technical information when you use them.

The app connects to the internet to check for and download updated word lists. As with any internet request, the server that delivers the lists receives your device's IP address and standard request information, such as the time of the request.

Web version

The web version at wordgarden.zombit.io is hosted by Vercel. It uses Vercel Web Analytics to measure visits and report aggregate statistics. Vercel states that it identifies visitors using a hash created from incoming requests rather than third-party cookies, and discards visitor sessions after 24 hours.

The page loads its font from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers under the Google Privacy Policy. Your progress and settings are kept in your browser's local storage. Sharing your results uses your device's share sheet or clipboard, and only when you choose to.

Location

The app does not collect precise information about your device's location.

Who else receives information

The services described above receive technical information when they deliver word lists, host the web version, provide web analytics, or serve fonts.

Your choices

To stop future requests, uninstall the mobile app or close the web version. To remove progress and settings stored by the web version, clear site data for wordgarden.zombit.io in your browser. These steps do not delete information that online services have already received.

For questions or deletion requests, email zombit.studios@gmail.com.
