This privacy policy applies to the Word Garden: Spelling Bee app (hereinafter referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, and to its web version at wordgarden.zombit.io, created by Zombit Studios LLC (hereinafter referred to as "Service Provider") as a Free service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not require registration. Progress and settings stay on your device or in your browser. Word-list requests and the web services described below receive technical information when you use them.

To check for and download updated word lists, the Application connects to the internet. As with any internet request, the server that delivers the lists receives your device's IP address and standard request information, such as the time of the request.

Web version

The web version at wordgarden.zombit.io is hosted by Vercel. It uses Vercel Web Analytics to measure visits and report aggregate statistics. Vercel states that it identifies visitors using a hash created from incoming requests rather than third-party cookies, and discards visitor sessions after 24 hours. The page loads its font from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers under the Google Privacy Policy. Your progress and settings are kept in your browser's local storage, and sharing your results uses your device's share sheet or clipboard only when you choose to.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Application does not collect precise information about the location of your mobile device.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

The services described above receive technical information when delivering word lists, hosting the web version, providing web analytics, or serving fonts.

What are my opt-out rights?

You can stop future requests from the mobile Application by uninstalling it, and from the web version by closing it. You can remove progress and settings stored by the web version by clearing site data for wordgarden.zombit.io in your browser. These actions do not delete information already received by online services. For questions or deletion requests, contact the Service Provider at zombit.studios@gmail.com.
