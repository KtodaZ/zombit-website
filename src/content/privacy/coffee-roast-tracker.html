The short version

This policy covers the Coffee Roast Tracker app for mobile devices, made by Zombit Studios LLC ("we"). The app is a paid download on the App Store and free on Google Play, and is provided "AS IS".

  • The app collects no information. You don't need to register.
  • The roasts you log are stored on your device. No data is shared with third parties.
  • If you buy the app on the App Store, Apple processes the payment. We don't receive your payment details.
  • There is nothing to opt out of. To stop using the app, uninstall it.
  • The app is not directed to children and collects no information from them.
  • Questions: email zombit.studios@gmail.com.

Opting out

The app collects no information, so there is no collection to opt out of. You can stop using the app at any time by uninstalling it.
