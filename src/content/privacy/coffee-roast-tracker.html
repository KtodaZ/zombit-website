The short version

This policy covers the Coffee Roast Tracker app for mobile devices, made by Zombit Studios LLC ("we"). The app is a paid download on the App Store and free on Google Play, and is provided "AS IS".

  • Questions: email zombit.studios@gmail.com.

Nothing. The app does not obtain any information when you download and use it. You don't need to register, and the roasts you log are stored on your device.

The app does not collect precise information about your device's location.

Third parties

The app collects no information, so no data is shared with third parties.

Opting out

The app collects no information, so there is no collection to opt out of. You can stop using the app at any time by uninstalling it.

Children

We encourage all children never to submit personally identifiable information through the app or our services. We encourage parents and legal guardians to monitor their children's internet use and to help enforce this policy by telling their children never to provide personally identifiable information through the app or our services without their permission.

If you have reason to believe a child has given us personally identifiable information through the app or our services, email zombit.studios@gmail.com so we can take the necessary action.

The roasts you log are stored on your device, and we don't collect them. Use your device's security features to help protect them from unauthorized access.
