The short version

This policy covers the Montessori Visual Timer app for iPhone, iPad, and Android devices, made by Zombit Studios LLC ("we"). The app is a paid download on the App Store and free on Google Play, and is provided "AS IS".

  • You don't need an account. The app never sends us your timer activity, settings, quick timers, or optional PIN. They are saved on your device, and your device's own backup (iCloud or your Google account) may include them.
  • The app has no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.
  • Feedback and store reviews are optional and meant for parents and guardians. Feedback reaches us through a form hosted by UserReport. Ratings are handled by the App Store or Google Play.
  • The app asks for notification and alarm permissions only to sound the timer's completion alert. It does not collect precise location.
  • Children can use the timer without submitting personal information online. Parents and guardians can ask us to review or delete their child's personal information.
  • To access or delete information you sent us, email zombit.studios@gmail.com.

The details are below.

What stays on your device

You don't need to register. The app does not send your timer activity or settings to us. Your settings, quick timers, and the optional PIN are saved only on your device and are never sent to us. Your device's own backup, such as iCloud or your Google account, may include them.

The app saves timer completion counts and your review-prompt choices on your device to manage rating reminders. On iOS, optional auto-rotation uses accelerometer readings on your device.

The app asks for notification and alarm permissions only to sound the timer's completion alert.

If you buy the app on the App Store, Apple processes the payment. We don't receive your payment details.

Location

The app does not collect precise information about your device's location.

Feedback, reviews, and third parties

The app does not share your locally saved timer data with third parties. It contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

Optional feedback and store reviews are meant for parents and guardians. The feedback link in Settings opens a form hosted by UserReport in your web browser. Anything you enter there is sent to us through that service, under UserReport's privacy policy. Requests to rate the app are handled by the App Store or Google Play, under Apple's or Google's privacy policy. Settings also includes an external privacy-policy link.

Your choices

You can stop using the app by uninstalling it. Uninstalling does not delete feedback already submitted through UserReport, store reviews, or existing device backups.

To request access to or deletion of information you submitted to us, email zombit.studios@gmail.com. Manage store reviews and device backups through Apple or Google.

Children

Children can use the timer without submitting personal information online. We don't use the app to knowingly ask children under 13 for data or to market to them, and we don't knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

We encourage all children never to submit personally identifiable information through the app or our services. We encourage parents and legal guardians to monitor their children's internet use and to help enforce this policy by telling their children never to provide personally identifiable information through the app or our services without their permission. Parents and guardians should handle optional feedback and store reviews and should not include children's personal information.

If you have reason to believe a child has given us personally identifiable information through the app or our services, email zombit.studios@gmail.com so we can take the necessary action.

Parents and legal guardians may ask to review or delete their child's personal information, or refuse its further collection or use, by contacting Zombit Studios LLC, 6852 Copper Glen Cir, Roseville, CA 95678-3449, United States; phone +1 279-226-9086; email zombit.studios@gmail.com.

Security

We care about keeping your information confidential. Local settings, including the optional PIN, rely on your device's security and backup protections. The PIN restricts timer controls; it does not encrypt your data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
